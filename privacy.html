Privacy statement

Last updated: 1 August 2026

This website (frewrites.com) is the personal and professional site of FRÉ. I care about your privacy and keep data collection to a minimum. This statement explains what data I process, why, and what your rights are, in line with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR / AVG).

Who is responsible

FRÉ (Frederike Berendsen) is the controller for the processing of your data on this website. You can reach me at fremusicofficial@gmail.com. Full details are on the legal notice page.

What I collect, and why

Newsletter (“Join my world”). If you sign up, I process your email address to send you occasional updates about new songs, shows and stories. Sign-up uses double opt-in: you confirm by clicking a link in a confirmation email, which is also your record of consent. Legal basis: your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR). You can unsubscribe at any time via the link in every email. The newsletter is sent with MailerLite, which processes the data on my behalf as a processor.

Contact. If you email me, I use the details you provide only to handle and answer your message. Legal basis: legitimate interest and, where relevant, taking steps at your request (Art. 6(1)(b) and (f) GDPR).

Server logs. The website is hosted by Cloudflare Pages. Like any web host, its servers briefly log technical data such as IP addresses to deliver the site securely and prevent abuse. Legal basis: legitimate interest in a safe, working website (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

What I do not do

  • No analytics, no tracking, no advertising pixels.
  • No tracking cookies are placed when you open the site.
  • Fonts and scripts are hosted on my own domain, so opening the site does not send your data to third parties.

Embedded video

Some pages embed video from YouTube in privacy-enhanced mode (youtube-nocookie). Nothing is loaded from YouTube until you press play. If you do, YouTube (Google) may process data under its own privacy policy.

Links to other services

The site links out to services such as streaming platforms, Patreon and Bandcamp. When you follow such a link, the privacy policy of that service applies. I have no control over their processing.

Transfers outside the EU

Some processors I use (MailerLite, Cloudflare, and YouTube if you play a video) are based in the United States. Where data is transferred outside the European Economic Area, it is protected by appropriate safeguards such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses and/or the EU–US Data Privacy Framework.

How long I keep data

Newsletter data is kept until you unsubscribe or ask me to remove it. Email correspondence is kept only as long as needed to handle your request. Server logs are kept for a short period by the host and then deleted.

Your rights

You have the right to access your data, to have it corrected or deleted, to restrict or object to processing, and to data portability. To exercise any of these, email fremusicofficial@gmail.com. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Dutch Data Protection Authority (Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens).

Changes

If anything about the way this site handles data changes, I will update this statement and the date at the top.
